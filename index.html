Need to host your image online? How to

  1. Go to My Gallery on Postimages.
  2. Click on your uploaded panorama image.
  3. Right-click the image and select "Open image in new tab".
  4. Copy the direct URL from the address bar (looks like https://i.postimg.cc/...) and paste it above.
